# Proposal figure and narrative notes

These notes support the supplied maximum-three-A4-page proposal outline; they are not a clinically validated research protocol.

## Central research question

Can NAT2 phenotype, clinical follow-up and explicit evidence applicability help prioritize monitoring attention during isoniazid therapy in Indonesian TB care?

## What the current prototype demonstrates

Four synthetic adult cases, independent exposure-concern indicators, deterministic monitoring priorities, inspectable rules and source-linked evidence limitations. A day 14 example changes TB-004's priority while its genotype stays fixed. Local storage preserves synthetic follow-ups within one browser. No patient outcomes or clinical performance have been measured.
