# Clinical fieldwork, made legible

The supplied brief fixes the visual direction: a light, restrained clinical tool. The app should feel like a carefully typeset research workstation, appropriate for a well-lit clinic and proposal screenshots.

White work surfaces, a cool off-white canvas, deep ink typography and a restrained forest-teal accent. Amber and rust communicate priority with explicit text labels. Use a self-hosted humanist sans face, tabular clinical values, fine rules, generous section separation and compact dense tables. No decorative imagery or invented medical illustrations.

The patient detail is the primary Operate surface: profile, genomic identity, dual concerns, reasoning and monitoring actions appear before supporting evidence. A stable genotype strip anchors a longitudinal baseline/follow-up comparison. The overview is a Persuade surface, with one clear patient-workspace action and a diagram that explains the mechanism. Evidence is a Read surface with inspectable sources and limitations.

Keyboard-visible focus, responsive navigation, reduced motion, labeled form controls, text status labels and horizontally scrollable data tables are required. Default body text is at least 14px; secondary text retains contrast. Cards represent meaningful groups and do not nest decorative cards. Interaction names describe the resulting action.
